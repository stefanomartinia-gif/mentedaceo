Mente da CEO Publisher

Mente da CEO Publisher is a personal tool used by the creator of the Mente da CEO pages to review short vertical videos and publish them to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. Short clips are prepared from material the creator owns or is authorized to use.
  2. The creator previews each clip, edits the caption and chooses the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure.
  3. Only after explicit confirmation is the video published through the official TikTok Content Posting API.
