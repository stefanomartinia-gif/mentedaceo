Mente da CEO Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Mente da CEO Publisher ("the App") is a personal publishing tool used by the creator behind the Mente da CEO accounts to publish their own short videos to their own TikTok account.

Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the App requests only the permissions needed to publish:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, shown so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • video.publish: to upload and publish videos that you have reviewed and approved.

How we use it

  • Your access token is used only to publish the videos you approve and to read the publishing status.
  • Tokens are stored locally on the creator's own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • We do not collect analytics, contacts, messages or any data about other TikTok users.

Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect the App at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services). Disconnecting revokes access, and the locally stored token is deleted.
